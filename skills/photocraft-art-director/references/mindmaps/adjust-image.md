# adjust-image

- `color.profileMismatch` — Resolves the embedded-profile-mismatch question after a file opens: keep the embedded profile, convert pixels to the working space, discard the profile, or assign the working one — use it when Color Settings ask-on-mismatch fires and a file's ICC profile differs from the working space.
- `image.adjustments.blackWhite` — Bakes a per-colour-weighted monochrome conversion into the active pixel layer; choose over desaturate when you want channel control (dark skies, glowing foliage) burned in — use layer.newAdjustmentLayer.blackWhite for a re-editable version.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.brightnessContrast` — Adds a non-destructive global light/dark + contrast layer above the active layer - the first choice for overall exposure nudges and punch.
- `image.adjustments.brightnessContrast` — Bakes a global tone lift/S-curve into the active pixel layer; choose for a fast value-nudge or value-step push on a whole layer — use Curves/Levels for targeted control or the adjustment-layer twin for re-editability.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.levels` — Adds a non-destructive black/white-point + gamma layer - the precise tool for setting true blacks, recovering washed-out range and midtone lift per channel.
- `image.adjustments.channelMixer` — Bakes a per-output-channel mix of the input channels plus a constant — the precision tool for fine B&W conversions, infrared and channel-swapped surreal colour; choose when hue/saturation shifts are too coarse.
- `image.autoTone` — Stretches the active pixel layer's tonal range per channel (0.1% clip) — choose for a fast, one-step fix for flat, hazy or dull photographs before any hand grading.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.curves` — Adds a non-destructive tone-curve layer (composite + per-channel) - the most powerful single grade: S-curves for drama, per-channel splits for cinematic colour.
- `image.adjustments.colorBalance` — Bakes per-tone-band colour pushes (shadows/midtones/highlights on the cyan-red, magenta-green, yellow-blue axes) into the active pixel layer; choose for split-tone grading — teal shadows + warm highlights — burned into one layer.
- `image.autoContrast` — Stretches tonal range with the SAME black/white points for all channels (0.1% clip), preserving colour balance — choose for fixing contrast without touching the colour cast, e.g. a sunset that must stay warm.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.exposure` — Adds a photographic stops-based exposure layer working in linear light - the honest way to simulate camera exposure, blown highlights and deep shade.
- `image.adjustments.colorLookup` — Applies a 3D colour-lookup table — a built-in film look (warm, tealOrange, crossProcess, …) or a .cube/.3dl/.look file — to the active pixel layer; choose for one-shot cinematic colour grading with film-emulation palettes.
- `image.autoColor` — Per-channel stretch plus midtone neutralisation (each channel's median pulled to the average median via per-channel gamma) — choose to remove colour casts and grey out a wrong white balance in one step.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.vibrance` — Adds a saturation layer that protects already-saturated colours and skin tones - the safe saturation boost when Hue/Saturation overcooks everything.
- `image.adjustments.colorLookup.list` — Returns the built-in Color Lookup look ids and labels; call before image.adjustments.colorLookup to pick a valid `lut` id.
- `image.imageSize` — Resamples every layer, mask, channel, selection, effect and vector to a new pixel size — choose for upscaling/downscaling artwork or photos, for print-size changes, or for hitting an exact export dimension.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.hueSaturation` — Adds a non-destructive hue/saturation/lightness layer with six editable colour ranges and a Colorize mode - the tool for recolouring one hue band or tinting monochrome work.
- `image.adjustments.curves` — Bakes spline tone curves (composite + per channel, 2..19 points in 0..255, or 256-level pencil maps) into the active pixel layer; the master tool for value shaping — S-curves, film toe/shoulder, per-channel cross-processed casts.
- `image.canvasSize` — Grows or shrinks the canvas WITHOUT resampling pixels — choose to add breathing room around artwork, extend a background for text, or make a matte/frame border.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.colorBalance` — Adds a shadows/midtones/highlights colour-shift layer on the red-cyan, green-magenta, blue-yellow axes - the classic grade for warm highlights, cool shadows.
- `image.adjustments.desaturate` — One-step bake of Hue/Saturation saturation -100 — neutral grey with no parameters; choose for a quick grey pass when per-channel B&W control is not needed.
- `image.crop` — Sets the canvas to an explicit rectangle (optionally rotated and/or resampled to a target size) or to the selection bounds — choose to tighten composition, straighten a horizon, or deliver an exact W x H at a ppi.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.blackWhite` — Adds a non-destructive monochrome layer where each colour's grey value is weighted by channel - how you make reds read dark or blues glow in a B&W image.
- `image.adjustments.equalize` — Histogram-equalizes the selection: remaps values by their cumulative distribution so all 256 levels get used; choose for maximum-contrast rescue of flat, foggy or scanned artwork — often harsh, so expect to dial back.
- `image.trim` — Crops away uniform border pixels — transparent edges by default, or edges matching a corner colour — choose to auto-tighten a flattened image, sprite or pasted artwork to its content.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.photoFilter` — Adds a coloured camera-glass filter layer with 20 presets (warming, cooling, underwater, sepia...) - the quick one-slider way to set a scene's colour temperature mood.
- `image.adjustments.exposure` — Bakes photographic stop-based exposure changes with offset and gamma; choose when the change is naturally measured in stops (whole-scene light shift) rather than slider units.
- `image.revealAll` — Grows the canvas just enough to show every layer's hidden pixels left outside after a deleteCroppedPixels:false crop — choose to recover content cut off by an earlier crop or moved layer.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.channelMixer` — Adds a layer that rebuilds each RGB output from weighted mixes of the input channels (+constant) - the power tool for surreal colour swaps and tuned B&W conversion.
- `image.adjustments.gradientMap` — Bakes a remap of every pixel's luminance through a gradient of 2..64 colour stops — the fastest duotone/tritone/poster-palette conversion; choose to recolour a value structure into a fixed palette.
- `image.perspectiveCrop` — Maps four corner points of a quad seen in perspective (a photographed document, poster, screen or facade) onto an upright W x H canvas in one undo step — choose to flatten skewed photos of flat art.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.colorLookup` — Adds a 3D LUT grade layer - pick one of 8 built-in film looks or load a .cube/.3dl/.look file for instant cinematic colour.
- `image.adjustments.hdrToning` — Local-adaptation tone mapping (edge-glow base/detail split in log luminance) for the grunge HDR look with pumped micro-contrast; WARNING: flattens the whole document to one locked Background and clears the selection.
- `image.rotation.arbitrary` — Rotates the entire canvas by any angle 0..359.99° cw or ccw, growing the canvas to fit — choose to tilt a whole artwork, or fine-straighten a horizon by a degree or two.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.invert` — Adds a layer that flips every colour to its negative - negative prints, x-ray looks, and the base for difference-style checks.
- `image.adjustments.hueSaturation` — Bakes global or per-hue-range hue rotation, saturation and lightness shifts (reds…magentas), plus a colorize mode for tinted monochrome; choose to retarget one colour family (only the sky blues) or re-key a palette.
- `image.imageRotation.90cw` — Turns the whole document exactly 90° clockwise with lossless pixel remapping (no interpolation) — choose for orientation fixes where quality must be untouched; canvas W/H swap.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.posterize` — Adds a layer that snaps each channel to N discrete levels - flat pop-art colour, cel-shaded looks, and palette reduction before quantising.
- `image.adjustments.invert` — Bakes 1-c on every channel (film negative); also inverts layer masks, alpha channels and Quick Masks when targeted — the cheap way to flip a reveal-mask into a hide-mask.
- `image.imageRotation.90ccw` — Turns the whole document exactly 90° counter-clockwise with lossless pixel remapping — the other quarter turn for orientation fixes; canvas W/H swap.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.threshold` — Adds a layer that snaps every pixel to pure black or white around a luma cut point - stark high-contrast graphics, and a live luminance-mask previewer.
- `image.adjustments.levels` — Bakes input black/gamma/white and output black/white remapping, composite and per channel; the surgical value tool — set black/white points, gamma-lift midtones, fade an image toward white via output levels.
- `image.imageRotation.180` — Turns the whole document upside down with lossless pixel remapping — choose to flip an inverted scan or check a composition's balance; canvas size unchanged.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.gradientMap` — Adds a layer that remaps each pixel's luminance onto a colour ramp of 2-64 stops - duotones, tritones and full palette skies driven by light.
- `image.adjustments.matchColor` — Transfers Lab colour statistics from a source document/layer onto the active pixel layer (Reinhard-style shift+scale), or just neutralizes a cast; choose to harmonize palettes between two artworks or versions.
- `image.imageRotation.flipCanvasHorizontal` — Mirrors the entire document left-right, losslessly — choose to flip a composition's flow (e.g. subject facing the other way) or check a painting with fresh eyes.
- `layer.newAdjustmentLayer.selectiveColor` — Adds a CMYK-ink nudge layer scoped to one colour range at a time (reds, greens, whites, blacks...) - surgical correction of exactly one hue family's ink balance.
- `image.adjustments.photoFilter` — Bakes a coloured filter wash (20 presets — warming85, cooling80, sepia, underwater — or a custom colour) at a density over the active pixel layer; choose for colour-temperature mood in one step.
- `image.imageRotation.flipCanvasVertical` — Mirrors the entire document top-bottom, losslessly — choose to build reflections (water, glass) from a source half, or correct vertically flipped material.
- `image.adjustments.posterize` — Bakes per-channel level quantization to N steps; choose for flat screenprint/risograph banding, stencil prep and deliberate stepped-gradients.
- `image.duplicate` — Makes a full copy of the active document as a new open document (optionally flattened to one layer) — choose for A/B variants, safe experiments, or a flattened delivery copy.
- `image.adjustments.replaceColor` — Builds a Color-Range-style mask around one colour (fuzziness) and bakes a hue/saturation/lightness shift weighted by that mask; choose to recolour a single colour family — a red car to blue, one petal hue to another.
- `image.applyImage` — Blends a channel/layer/document source onto the active layer's pixels with a blend mode, opacity, optional mask and preserve-transparency — choose for channel-driven effects: luminosity sharpening, glow from a channel, hand-made HDR, texture overlay.
- `image.adjustments.selectiveColor` — Bakes CMYK-ink corrections targeted at one of 9 colour ranges (reds…blacks) in relative or absolute mode; choose for ink-level fixes — rich blacks, clean neutrals, print-oriented colour steering.
- `image.calculations` — Blends two channel sources (any documents of the same size) with a blend mode into a NEW alpha channel, a new grayscale document, or the selection — choose to build masks and selections from channel math: sky masks, luminosity keys, texture plates.
- `image.adjustments.shadowsHighlights` — Edge-aware shadow lift and highlight recovery with tone masking, colour correction and a midtone S-curve — measured against Photoshop 25.4; choose to open crushed shadows or rescue skies without flattening global contrast.
- `image.trap` — Spreads inks at colour boundaries on a CMYK image to prevent white gaps from press misregistration — choose only for print-prep of CMYK artwork with hard colour edges.
- `image.adjustments.threshold` — Bakes a hard black/white split of the luminance at one level; choose for stencils, high-contrast B&W graphics and mask extraction — every intermediate value is gone.
- `image.variables.define` — Binds named variables to a layer's visibility, text, or pixel content — the first half of data-driven graphics: define the template slots once, then feed rows of data (choose when batch-rendering name badges, banners, card variants).
- `image.adjustments.vibrance` — Bakes a saturation boost that favours muted pixels (and damps skin/reds) plus an optional plain saturation pass; choose for a colour punch that will not torch already-vivid areas.
- `image.variables.dataSets` — Stores named data sets (rows of variable values) in the document — the second half of data-driven graphics: one row per rendered variant (choose together with image.variables.define).
- `image.applyDataSet` — Applies one stored data set to the document — sets layer visibility, swaps text, replaces pixel layers from image files — one history step per variant (choose to render/preview a template row; batch-export with file.export.dataSetsAsFiles).
- `layer.setAdjustment` — Edits an existing adjustment layer's parameters in place — params merge over current values, a call with only `layer` resets to defaults; the Properties-panel write path (one drag = one undo step).
- `image.mode.bitmap` — Converts a Grayscale image to 1-bit black/white by threshold, pattern dither, diffusion dither or a halftone screen — choose it for true 1-bit artwork, newsprint/halftone grit or stark posterised silhouettes.
- `image.mode.bits16` — Converts the document to 16 bits per channel — choose it for heavy grading, curves and dodge/burn work on scans or RAW-derived photos where 8-bit would band.
- `image.mode.bits32` — Converts the document to 32-bit floating point holding linear light — choose it for HDR compositing, exposure manipulation and light-accurate blends above 100% white.
- `image.mode.bits8` — Converts the document to 8 bits per channel — the standard delivery depth; choose it when exporting to web/social formats or when 16/32-bit headroom is no longer needed.
- `image.mode.cmyk` — Converts the document to CMYK through the CMS (working CMYK or a chosen profile) — choose it when preparing print-ready separations or when matching an offset press space.
- `image.mode.colorTable` — Edits the palette of an Indexed Color image: swap in a preset ramp, recolour individual entries, or set the transparent index — choose it for palette-swap art, retro ramps and GIF-style transparency.
- `image.mode.duotone` — Turns a Grayscale image into a monotone/duotone/tritone/quadtone ink mix — choose it for two-ink print looks, sepia/selenium-style toning or branded monochrome posters.
- `image.mode.grayscale` — Converts the document to grayscale through the CMS (working gray or a chosen profile) — choose it for true monochrome documents with ICC-managed tone (not a plain desaturate).
- `image.mode.indexedColor` — Quantises the flattened image to a 2..256-colour palette with dithering — choose it for GIF-style web graphics, retro palettes and palette-constrained illustration.
- `image.mode.lab` — Converts the document to CIELAB, separating lightness from colour — choose it for lightness-only retouching, punchy colour boost on L, or device-independent colour work.
- `image.mode.multichannel` — Flattens the image into raw spot/ink channels (RGB → C/M/Y, CMYK → C/M/Y/K, Lab → Alpha 1–3, Grayscale → Black, Duotone → its inks) — choose it for spot-colour plate work or pure channel data without a colour model.
- `image.mode.rgb` — Converts the document to RGB through the CMS (working RGB or a chosen profile) — choose it to bring any mode back to screen space, or to move between RGB gamuts (sRGB ↔ Display P3 ↔ ProPhoto) for delivery.
- `view.gamutWarning` — Overlays the canvas pixels a proof profile cannot reproduce (out-of-gamut mask) and reports how many pixels are affected — toggle it before print conversion to see what a printer cannot hit.
- `view.proofColors` — Toggles on-canvas simulation of the Proof Setup profile (default coated CMYK) via the display LUT — switch it on to preview how the artwork will print without converting the document.
- `view.proofSetup` — Chooses which profile the canvas simulates when Proof Colors is on — a CMYK press profile, a RGB space, or any .icc file; use it to target a specific printer before grading.
- `view.proofSetup.colorBlindnessDeuteranopia` — Simulates green-weak (deuteranope) vision on the canvas and turns Proof Colors on — check charts, UI art and infographics stay legible for the most common colour-blindness type.
- `view.proofSetup.colorBlindnessProtanopia` — Simulates red-weak (protanope) vision on the canvas and turns Proof Colors on — the stricter check for red-based encodings, since protanopes also see red much darker.
- `view.proofSetup.legacyMacintoshRgb` — Shows the document's RGB numbers on a gamma-1.8 display (numbers preserved, sRGB primaries) and turns Proof Colors on — the classic 'unmanaged old Mac' brighter-midtone look, for matching legacy artwork.
- `view.proofSetup.workingBlackPlate` — Previews only the black plate of the working CMYK separation, printed as gray ink, and turns Proof Colors on — check K coverage on text, shadows and outline art before sending separations.
- `view.proofSetup.workingCmyPlate` — Previews cyan+magenta+yellow inks with the black plate left out, and turns Proof Colors on — check how much of the image leans on K, or how a no-black print would look.
- `view.proofSetup.workingCyanPlate` — Previews only the cyan plate of the working CMYK separation as gray ink, and turns Proof Colors on — inspect cyan coverage in skies, water and cool areas.
- `view.proofSetup.workingMagentaPlate` — Previews only the magenta plate of the working CMYK separation as gray ink, and turns Proof Colors on — inspect magenta coverage in skin tones, reds and purples.
- `view.proofSetup.workingYellowPlate` — Previews only the yellow plate of the working CMYK separation as gray ink, and turns Proof Colors on — inspect yellow coverage in greens, golds and highlights.
- `view.thirtyTwoBitPreviewOptions` — Sets how a 32-bit (HDR) document previews on screen: exposure in stops and gamma applied in linear light, or highlight compression — use it to judge HDR content on an SDR display, e.g. +1 stop to check shadow detail.
